Examiner / Viva Mode — How This App Works

  • Full-Stack Setup: Simple webpage (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) talks to a Python FastAPI server, and saves data in a local SQLite database file (arogyanav.db).
  • Hospital Ranking Rules: Starts at 50, adds points when the hospital fits the visit type (checkup, tests, or staying for treatment), and adds higher points for Ayushman card support during hospital stays while noting it mainly covers hospital stays rather than routine OPD visits.
  • Login & Saved Choices: Passwords are safely scrambled (hashed) before saving in SQLite, and your chosen city and card stay saved for next time.
  • Cheaper Medicine Match: Matches costly brand names to their main medicine ingredient (salt) so families can ask for lower-cost generic options.
